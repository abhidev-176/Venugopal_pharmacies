Common questions

Before you order

Do I need a prescription for every medicine?

No — only for Rx-marked items. Everyday medicines like paracetamol or antacids don't need one.

How fast is delivery?

15–30 minutes within the local service area, longer for outlying addresses.

Can I pay on delivery?

Yes, along with UPI and card payment options at checkout.

What if a medicine is out of stock?

We call you with the nearest available alternative before substituting anything.

Whether it's a monthly refill for a family member or a one-off need at midnight, Abhi Medical Stores is built to treat both with the same seriousness.
